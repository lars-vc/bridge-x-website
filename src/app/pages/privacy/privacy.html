2. What personal data do we process?

We only process the personal data that is necessary for the purposes described below.

This may include:

  • first and last name;

We do not request special categories of personal data and ask you not to provide such information through this website.

3. Why do we use your personal data?

We use your personal data only for clearly defined purposes, including:

Contact and responding to enquiries

When you contact us through a CTA, contact form or email, we use your information to understand and respond to your enquiry, communicate with you about your request, provide further information where relevant, and discuss a potential business relationship or collaboration.

Depending on the circumstances, the legal basis for this processing is either taking steps at your request prior to entering into a contract or our legitimate interest in responding to and conducting business communications.

Website operation and security

Limited technical information may be processed when you visit our website where this is necessary to ensure its proper functioning and security.

This may include technical information such as your IP address, date and time of access, browser or device information and technical log data. This information is not used to profile visitors or track their behaviour across different websites.

4. No tracking or profiling

Bridge-X does not use this website for commercial tracking or profiling.

We do not use:

  • advertising trackers;
  • retargeting technologies;
  • social media tracking pixels;
  • behavioural profiling;
  • the sale of personal data to third parties.

We also do not use analytical cookies to individually track visitors' browsing behaviour.

5. Email communication

When you send us an email or contact us through a CTA or contact form, we retain the information provided for as long as necessary to handle your enquiry and any relevant follow-up communication.

We do not automatically use your email address to send you newsletters or marketing communications.

If we wish to send you marketing communications in the future where your consent is legally required, we will ask for your consent in the appropriate manner.

6. How long do we keep your data?

We do not retain personal data for longer than necessary for the purpose for which it was collected.

As a general guideline:

  • contact and CTA enquiries: for as long as necessary to handle the enquiry and any relevant follow-up;
  • business correspondence: for as long as reasonably necessary for the business relationship or to establish or defend our rights and obligations;
  • data subject to a legal retention obligation: for the period required by applicable law.

When personal data is no longer required, it will be deleted or anonymised unless a legal obligation requires us to retain it for longer.

7. Who do we share your data with?

We do not sell or rent your personal data to third parties.

Your data may only be processed by service providers supporting Bridge-X in the operation of its website and business communications, such as:

  • hosting and IT service providers;
  • email and communication service providers;
  • website security or maintenance providers;
  • accountancy provider.

These parties process personal data only to the extent necessary to provide their services and, where required, on the basis of appropriate contractual arrangements.

8. Your rights

Under applicable data protection legislation, including the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), you may, depending on the circumstances, have the right to:

  • request access to your personal data;
  • have inaccurate personal data corrected;
  • request the deletion of your personal data;
  • request restriction of processing;
  • object to certain processing activities;
  • receive your personal data in a portable format where the right to data portability applies;
  • withdraw consent where processing is based on your consent.

You can exercise your rights by contacting us at kattyvancamp@bridge-x.be.

To protect your privacy, we may reasonably ask you to verify your identity before responding to your request.

We will respond within the applicable legal timeframe. In principle, this is one month, although this period may be extended in certain circumstances as permitted by the GDPR.
